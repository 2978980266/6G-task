Vehicle CSV columns

recordTimeUtc: real-world record time in UTC. Unit: ISO datetime
sessionSeconds: elapsed time since capture started. Unit: second
simulationFrame: game simulation frame. Unit: frame
gameTime: in-game time. Unit: in-game datetime
vehicleId: tracked vehicle id. Unit: id
x: vehicle world position on X axis. Unit: meter
y: vehicle world position on Y axis, usually height. Unit: meter
z: vehicle world position on Z axis. Unit: meter
angleX: vehicle pitch angle. Unit: degree
angleY: vehicle heading angle. Unit: degree
speed: vehicle speed magnitude. Unit: meter per second
prefabName: vehicle prefab/template name. Unit: text
